01 / 03 · HOME

The first fold does five things at once.

Location, greeting, search, AI hook, and category grid — all above the fold, none competing for attention. The trick is the AI banner: it's the darkest thing on the page, so the eye lands there without being pushed.

The AI banner is the hero

Deep navy against warm paper, a single emerald sparkle, and copy that names the outcome ("Fair price in 30 seconds"). It's the one glowing thing on a calm page — you can't miss it.

Each service tile has its own personality

Electrician is deep blue (industrial), Plumber is teal (water), Heating is warm orange (fire), Gardener is forest green. Colour does the work of remembering — you learn the shapes.

Trust in numbers, not badges

"2,500+ RECI verified" beats a generic "Trusted" seal. The strip uses the display serif for the numbers so they read like answers, not decoration.

02 / 03 · Describe & Price

The moment the price appears.

Two photos and a sentence in; a fair price band out. This is the single interaction that separates FairFix from every other Irish tradesman platform.

02 / 03 · DESCRIBE & PRICE

A range, a confidence score, a receipt.

The AI never diagnoses the fault (that would create liability). It shows the price range similar jobs settled at, with a confidence percentage and a sample size — a fair, verifiable, non-promissory answer.

The estimate card is dark on purpose

Everything else on the screen is warm paper. The estimate is deep navy with an emerald glow — the app's most important object gets its most important treatment.

€
Numbers wear the serif

€90 – €160. The Old Style serif shows up here for the first time on the screen — that visual weight tells the user this is the answer, not a suggestion.

Photo slots that actually look like photos

Filled slots show soft, colourful placeholder gradients so the user knows they've done the step. Empty slots stay dashed and quiet. No stock icons pretending to be images.

03 / 03 · MATCHES

Choosing a person, not scrolling through spam.

Bark shows twenty. Airtasker shows fifty. FairFix shows three, one of them highlighted. Fewer, better, faster — that's the whole promise on this screen.

Avatars have colour, not photos

Initials on branded gradients. No stock headshots, no privacy issues, no cold-start problem when a new tradesman joins without a photo.

The Top Match earns its glow

An emerald ring and a soft green wash. Not everyone gets highlighted — one card, one moment of "here's my pick." Removes the paralysis of three good choices.

Counters show reasoning

When Sean asks for €25 more, he explains why. The message sits on an amber left rule so it reads as "attention" — not a complaint, an invitation to consider.

Illustration language

Colourful, categorical, calm.

Every category earns its own palette. Reused across tiles, badges, empty states and marketing — one system, everywhere.

The palette

Eight colours. Every one has a job.

Not a rainbow. Every hex earns its place — navy for authority, emerald for progress, coral only for emergencies, amber only for earned attention. Nothing wanders.
